About LocalConnect

Connecting local businesses, citizens and community welfare initiatives through one simple digital platform.

Join Our Community

Who We Are

LocalConnect is a web-based community platform designed to bring people, local businesses and welfare services together. It provides a common place where residents can explore local products, report public park problems, discover animal adoption opportunities and find support services for senior citizens.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage community participation, promote small businesses and make local welfare services easier to discover. We aim to use technology to improve communication between residents and community initiatives.

Our Vision

We envision a connected community where residents support local entrepreneurs, care for public spaces, help animals and assist people who need additional support.

Our Main Objectives

What LocalConnect aims to achieve

🛍️

Support Local Business

Help people discover local products and encourage small businesses.

🌳

Encourage residents to report park maintenance issues and care for shared spaces.

💚

Encourage animal welfare, volunteering and support for senior citizens.
